# AstraPath: MVP doc and pages

AstraPath is an indoor guide that takes you from a building's front door to the exact room. See [mvp_doc.md](mvp_doc.md) for the MVP doc.

## Pages

| Page | URL | Audience | Value prop |
|---|---|---|---|
| Homepage | https://astrapath-indoornav.netlify.app/ | Anyone finding a room in a building they don't know | The right room, not just the right building: which entrance, hallway, elevator, and door |
| Students | https://astrapath-indoornav.netlify.app/students/ | Students in a building they haven't been inside, between classes | Which hallway, which stairwell, which elevator, with ten minutes to spare |
| Visitors | https://astrapath-indoornav.netlify.app/visitors/ | Visitors with appointments, interviews, or office visits | The right door: the entrance visitors use, where to check in, the elevator, the room |
| Step-free | https://astrapath-indoornav.netlify.app/step-free/ | People who need a step-free route | A step-free route of its own, with the elevator and the floors it reaches named |
| Venues | https://astrapath-indoornav.netlify.app/venues/ | People who run buildings | Free directions for every visitor; affordable plans priced to the building |

Every page has one call to action: join the waitlist. After signing up, people land on the thank-you page: https://astrapath-indoornav.netlify.app/thanks/
