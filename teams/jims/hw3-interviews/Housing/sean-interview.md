# NYC Housing Interview Guide

## Intro note

> Thanks for making time. We're Columbia students looking into what it's really like to find housing when moving to New York. There are no right or wrong answers. We just want to hear your story, so feel free to go into as much detail as you like.

## Getting started

1. Where were you before, and what brought you to New York?
2. Walk me through your move, from the moment you knew you were coming to the day you got your keys.
3. What was the first thing you did when you started looking for a place?

## The search

4. What did a typical week of searching look like for you?
5. Where did you look? Which one did you end up using the most, and why?
6. Which sources did you trust, and which didn't you? What made the difference?
7. Did you ever come across a scam or a listing that wasn't what it seemed? What happened?
8. Roughly how many places did you look at? How did you keep track of them?
9. Did looking at all these different places feel overwhelming? What made it feel that way?
10. How did you narrow it down? What made you drop a place or keep it?
11. What information did you wish listings had?
12. How did you figure out if a place or neighborhood was good without seeing it?
13. Did you ever lose a place because someone else moved faster?
14. Was there a moment you felt stuck? Was there a moment it finally clicked?

## Getting the place

15. Tell me about applying and getting approved. What paperwork did they ask for?
16. International: Did not having US credit, an SSN, or a guarantor come up? How did you handle it?

    Domestic: Did your credit, income, or guarantor come up? How did you handle it?

## Costs

17. How did the final cost compare to what you expected? Were there any surprise fees?

## Looking back

18. Where did you stay before your lease started?
19. What was the hardest part of the whole experience?
20. What would you do differently, or tell a friend moving here next month?

## Close

21. Is there anything you wished about the process that could have made it easier for you? Any resource, website, person that could have helped, anything?
22. Is there anything we didn't ask that we should have? Do you know anyone else we could talk to?
